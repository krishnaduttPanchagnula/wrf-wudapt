## Plan , design and operate , from buildings to cities

One engine gives the same results at each step: the city plan, the building design and the daily operation.

- 01 · Plan Plan districts and cities Find hot streets and windy corners across a whole city. Then set the rules for new projects.
- 02 · Design Design better buildings Compare options for sun, daylight, wind and perceived heat (UTCI) while the design can still change. Example: Singapore tower study.
- 03 · Operate Operate with a live layer A live layer for digital twins: the climate forecast for the next 72 hours, every hour, at metre scale. Example: ice on the streets of Luleå.
