## What one simulation costs

Every simulation is paid in tokens from your plan. The SDK and the platform show the count before you run, free of charge. On a plan, 10 tokens cost €0.17 (Pro) to €0.40 (Starter).

**Thermal comfort tiers.** Standard: Sun, shade and the heat of walls and pavements, with the wind of the weather file. Advanced with local wind: Adds a heat lag for each ground material and the wind street by street from our AI wind model. The wind runs are included.

## Questions

**Can I start without paying?**

Yes. A new account gets 900 tokens once, with no credit card. They are valid for 42 days.

**Do unused plan tokens carry over?**

Yes. They stay valid until the end of your paid year.

**Can I buy more tokens?**

Yes. Buy a top-up pack on the platform. The top-up price follows your plan.

**Do I pay twice if a run fails?**

No. A repeated request after a network error is paid once. A failed AI workflow gives your tokens back.
